Course Overview
The Basic to Advanced IT course provides a comprehensive foundation in essential computer skills and digital tools. Designed for beginners and those looking to strengthen their IT knowledge, this course covers everything from fundamental hardware and software concepts to advanced skills in office productivity, graphic design, and web development. Students will also explore modern tools like AI and enhance their typing speed through structured practice.
Course Prerequisites
Basic computer literacy
No prior technical experience required
What We Learn in This Course
Introduction to Computer Hardware
Understanding Computer Software
Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
Graphic Designing using Canva
Web Development with HTML & CSS
Typing Practice with Typing Master
Introduction to Artificial Intelligence using ChatGPT
