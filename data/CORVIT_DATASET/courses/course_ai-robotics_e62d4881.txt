Course Overview
The Robotics course offers a comprehensive introduction to the principles and applications of robotics. Students will explore the fundamentals of robot design, control, and programming. The course covers various types of robots, including mobile robots and robotic arms, and their use in different industries. Practical sessions will provide hands-on experience in building and programming robots.
Course Prerequisites
Basic understanding of programming concepts
Familiarity with basic electronics
Knowledge of physics and mathematics
What We Learn in This Course
Introduction to robotics and its applications
Robot design and construction
Programming robotic systems
Control systems and sensors
Robotics in industrial applications
Hands-on projects in robotics
