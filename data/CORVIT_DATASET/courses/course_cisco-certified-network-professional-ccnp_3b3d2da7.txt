Cisco Certified Network Professional (CCNP)
Curriculum
Course Overview:
The
Cisco Certified Network Professional
course provides a deep dive into advanced networking concepts and technologies. This course is designed for professionals seeking to enhance their skills and knowledge in network infrastructure, routing, switching, and security. Participants will learn how to design, implement, and troubleshoot complex network solutions, preparing them for the CCNP certification exams and advanced networking roles.
Course Prerequisites:
Basic understanding of networking concepts and protocols.
Completion of the CCNA (Cisco Certified Network Associate) certification or equivalent experience.
Familiarity with IP addressing and subnetting.
What We Learn in This Course:
Advanced Routing:
In-depth exploration of routing protocols and techniques, including OSPF, EIGRP, and BGP.
Advanced Switching:
Configuring and managing advanced switching features, including VLANs, STP, and EtherChannel.
Network Security:
Implementing and managing network security measures, including firewalls, VPNs, and access control.
Network Design:
Designing scalable and resilient network infrastructures.
Troubleshooting:
Techniques for diagnosing and resolving network issues.
Quality of Service (QoS):
Implementing QoS policies to ensure optimal network performance.
