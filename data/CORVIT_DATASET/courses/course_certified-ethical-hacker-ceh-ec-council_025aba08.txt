Course Overview
The Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) course provides a deep dive into ethical hacking practices. Students will learn to identify and exploit vulnerabilities in various systems and networks, all while understanding the ethical implications. This course prepares students for the CEH certification and equips them with the skills to protect organizations from cyber threats.
Course Prerequisites
Basic understanding of networking concepts
Knowledge of operating systems
Familiarity with programming languages is beneficial
What We Learn in This Course
Introduction to ethical hacking
Footprinting and reconnaissance
Scanning networks
Enumeration techniques
System hacking methodologies
Malware threats and analysis
Hands-on ethical hacking practices
