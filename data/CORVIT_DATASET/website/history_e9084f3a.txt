History Of Corvit ​
Overview
For last 25+ years, CORVIT has commanded respect in the IT Industry not only in Pakistan but in the entire Middle East Region and beyond. CORVIT is committed to the cause of providing exceptional technical trainings and solutions to its customer across the globe.
For the past two decades, we have trained and produced highly skilled Professionals for the local and global industry. Our students are working and making contributions to the IT industry across the world. We also provide technology solutions to our customers across various verticals. This gives an extra edge to our training programs, whereby we are able to impart practical knowledge to our students. As a result, our students develop into true professionals, ready to take on the real-world challenges.
History
Founded in year 2000 as a provider of IT Trainings, Products, Services and professional HR resources, CORVIT has rapidly evolved into a trusted business / technology advisor and a strategic partner for our customers. Our solution offerings span the entire spectrum of Information and Communication Technologies (ICT). We are ISO 9001 certified company and aim to offer IT trainings, solutions and services tailored to empower our customers and lower the TCO (Total Cost of Ownership) for their Technology investments.
Experience
Learning with CORVIT means real-world experience that helps students transition from classroom to job, with ease. Since our instructors have in depth understanding of cutting-edge technologies, students are guaranteed to gain knowledge and skills to enhance their expertise. We also offer a variety of foundation courses to help prepare our students to eventually be able to learn more advanced and emerging technologies. We are committed to help our students build foundations for exciting and promising IT careers.
As already mentioned, CORVIT offers numerous ways for our customers to get the training they need. Our Instructor-Led Trainings (ILT), Instructor-Led Online (ILO) Trainings, and Blended Classroom / Virtual training courses utilize authorized Vendor and custom developed curriculum. In addition to our extensive public course schedule, we provide private group, on-site raining at customers’ choice of location. Our instructors are certified by multiple Vendors, and most use their expert knowledge to contribute heavily to our new course development projects.
