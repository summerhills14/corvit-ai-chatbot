CEH Certification in Pakistan 2026 — Career Paths, Salaries, and How to Get Started
By Corvit Systems · Rawalpindi Campus · June 2026 · 6 min read
Cyber attacks on Pakistani organizations — from government databases to banking systems — have made headlines repeatedly over the past two years. The result? Demand for qualified cybersecurity professionals has surged, and the Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) certification from EC-Council has emerged as one of the most valuable credentials a Pakistani IT professional can hold in 2026.
If you are asking “should I do the CEH?” or “what job will it get me?” — this post answers all of it, including the specific July 2026 batches available right now at Corvit Systems Rawalpindi.
What Is the CEH Certification?
The Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) is an internationally recognized certification by EC-Council. It validates your ability to think like a hacker — understanding attack methodologies, vulnerabilities, and penetration testing — while using those skills ethically and legally to defend organizations.
CEH covers areas including:
Footprinting and reconnaissance
Scanning networks and system hacking
Malware threats and social engineering
Web application security and SQL injection
Cloud and IoT security
Cryptography and network packet analysis
CEH is not just a theory exam — it is a practical, lab-heavy certification that employers treat as evidence you can actually perform security assessments, not just describe them.
Career Paths After CEH in Pakistan
Here are the most common roles Pakistani CEH holders land:
Ethical Hacker / Penetration Tester
PKR 80,000 – 200,000/mo
Contracted to test security of networks and applications. High demand from banks, telcos, government.
SOC Analyst (L1 / L2)
PKR 60,000 – 150,000/mo
Monitor networks for threats and respond to incidents. Entry-level roles widely available.
Security Consultant
PKR 120,000 – 300,000/mo
Advise organizations on security posture. Remote/international work common.
Network Security Engineer
PKR 90,000 – 220,000/mo
Design and maintain secure network infrastructure. Natural next step from CCNA.
Freelance Security Auditor
$500 – $3,000/project
International clients on Upwork and Fiverr. USD earnings increasingly popular.
Cybersecurity Trainer
PKR 80,000 – 180,000/mo
Teach ethical hacking at institutes. Demand growing as the field expands.
Who Should Take the CEH Course at Corvit?
You are a good fit for CEH if you:
Already have a background in networking (CCNA or equivalent) and want to move into security
Are working in IT and want to add a high-value certification recognized internationally
Want to freelance on cybersecurity projects internationally
Are a fresh graduate with a strong interest in security wanting a market-ready credential
Work in a bank, telecom, or government body and want to understand how your systems are attacked
Prerequisite:
CEH works best with some networking knowledge. If you don’t have a CCNA or networking background, consider doing CCNA first — Corvit offers multiple CCNA batches starting in July.
CEH vs SOC Analyst — Which to Choose?
Both are cybersecurity career paths but different in focus. CEH is
offensive security
— learning how attacks work by simulating them. SOC Analyst is
defensive security
— monitoring, detecting, and responding to real attacks. They are complementary. Many security professionals do both over time. If you want a job faster, SOC Analyst roles are more available at entry level. If you want higher long-term earning potential and international freelance work, CEH opens more doors.
Corvit offers both in July 2026. You can even combine them.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long does the CEH course take?
At Corvit, the CEH course runs for approximately 2 months with regular lab sessions included.
Does Corvit prepare me for the official EC-Council CEH exam?
Yes. The Corvit CEH syllabus follows the official EC-Council CEH v12 outline, preparing you fully for the certification exam.
Can I take CEH without a networking background?
It is possible but challenging. We recommend at least a basic understanding of networking concepts. If needed, you can take CCNA first at Corvit.
Is CEH recognized internationally?
Yes. EC-Council CEH is recognized globally and accepted by employers across the GCC, UK, and North America.
